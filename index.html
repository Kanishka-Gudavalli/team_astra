WHO WE ARE

Pushing the Boundaries
of Aerial Innovation

Team Astra is a dynamic, multidisciplinary student team from IIITDM Kancheepuram specializing in designing, building, and operating Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) and drones.

As a leading technical club under the institute's SAE Collegiate Club, we compete in national aeromodelling competitions, focusing on mechanical design, electronics integration, and software development for advanced drone technology.

Our team brings together passionate engineers across multiple disciplines, united by the vision of advancing autonomous aerial systems and solving real-world challenges through cutting-edge UAV technology.

Mechanical

Airframe design, aerodynamics & structural analysis

Electronics

Power systems, flight controllers & sensor fusion

Software

Autonomous navigation, computer vision & control
